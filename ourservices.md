<h2>Data Privacy &amp; Compliance</h2>
<p>Sustainable privacy programs designed for your business, your industry, and the jurisdictions you operate in.</p>
</div>
<div class="service-body" markdown="1">
### Services

- Privacy program development and implementation
- Data mapping and records of processing
- Data protection and privacy impact assessments
- Data governance strategy
- Privacy notices and internal policies
- Data subject and consumer rights processes
- Website tracking, cookie, and consent compliance
- Privacy-by-design reviews for products and features
- Privacy training for leadership and staff

### Laws and frameworks

- EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- US state privacy laws, including the CCPA/CPRA
- Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)
- Driver's Privacy Protection Act (DPPA)
- Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA and Québec's Law 25
- Latin American data protection laws, including Brazil's LGPD
- Southeast Asian data protection laws, including Singapore's PDPA
- Biometric privacy laws
- ISO/IEC 27701

<section class="service" id="ai" markdown="1">
<div class="service-title">
<h2>AI Governance &amp; Regulation</h2>
<p>Helping organizations adopt and build AI responsibly, with governance that tracks fast-moving and still-developing rules.</p>
</div>
<div class="service-body" markdown="1">
### Services

- AI governance programs, policies, and oversight structures
- AI use-case inventories and risk classification
- AI risk and impact assessments
- EU AI Act readiness, including role and risk-tier analysis
- Transparency, human oversight, and accountability controls
- AI vendor diligence and contract terms
- Privacy and data protection issues in model training and deployment
- AI training for boards, executives, and product teams

### Regulatory requirements

- EU Artificial Intelligence Act
- US state AI and automated decision-making laws and regulations
- Privacy-law rules on profiling and automated decisions, including under the GDPR

### Voluntary frameworks and standards

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management systems)

<div class="note" markdown="1">
AI rules are moving quickly, and many are proposed, phased in, or subject to change. We help clients separate binding obligations from voluntary best practices and plan for both.
</div>
</div>
</section>
