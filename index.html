How we work

Experienced counsel, built around how you work.

Fixed-fee engagements

Most of our work is offered on a fixed-fee basis. You know the cost before we start, and our incentive is to be efficient, not to bill hours.

Cross-border by default

Data doesn't stop at borders. We address each jurisdiction's requirements on its own terms, then build one program that works across all of them.
